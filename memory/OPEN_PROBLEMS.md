# GSMH Open Problems

No research cycle has been run yet. The following foundational items remain open:

1. Define GSMH precisely, including fields, parameters, action or postulates, and boundary conditions.
2. Derive the equations of motion and establish covariance, constraints, and conservation laws.
3. Determine the propagating degrees of freedom and test for ghosts, instabilities, and causality violations.
4. Derive controlled GR, weak-field, and Newtonian limits.
5. Construct reproducible symbolic and numerical tests, including perturbations and parameter sensitivity.
6. Compare predictions with Solar System, binary-pulsar, cosmological, lensing, galaxy, and cluster observations.
